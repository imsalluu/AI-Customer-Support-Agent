# SupportIQ AI — Commercial Customer Pitch & Sales Deck
**Enterprise AI-Powered Customer Support Automation Platform**

## Executive Summary

**SupportIQ AI** is an autonomous B2B SaaS platform that automates customer support across **Website Chat**, **WhatsApp Cloud API**, and **Inbound Email**.

Unlike standard legacy chatbots that guess or hallucinate answers, SupportIQ AI connects directly to real-time company databases (orders, inventory, customer history) and semantic knowledge bases using **pgvector RAG**. Every response is 100% grounded in verified data with exact source citations and automated human-in-the-loop escalation.

## The Problem: Why Support is Broken Today

1. **Skyrocketing Support Costs:** High-growth e-commerce & SaaS companies spend $3,500+ per support agent/month.
2. **Frustrated Customers:** 78% of customers abandon checkout or leave negative reviews when support takes longer than 5 minutes to respond after hours.
3. **Chatbot Hallucinations:** Generic AI bots invent fake discount codes, forge order tracking, and give incorrect return policies.
4. **Agent Burnout:** Support agents spend 85% of their working hours answering repetitive questions ("Where is my order?", "What is your return policy?").

## Key Commercial Differentiators

### 1. 11 Controlled Business Tools (Zero-Hallucination Guarantee)
The AI never guesses store status. When a customer asks *"Where is my order ORD-10022?"*, the AI calls `get_order_status("ORD-10022")`, retrieves the verified carrier tracking status, and formats the reply instantly.

### 2. pgvector Knowledge Retrieval with Exact Citations
Ingest return policies, shipping FAQs, user manuals, and warranty documentation. The AI retrieves semantic chunks and generates answers citing exact document titles, sections, and page numbers.

### 3. Real-Time Human Handoff & Sentiment Escalation
If a customer message expresses anger or high frustration, the system immediately halts automated generation, generates an urgent support ticket, and alerts human support specialists to take over.
